# Rewrite protocol

## Before drafting

Infer the format, locale, discipline, role, reader knowledge, and stance. Relevant roles include enthusiast, mechanic, coach, racer, commuter, tourer, and editor. Relevant stances include recommendation, diagnosis, comparison, report, opinion, and instruction.

## Fact lock

Preserve product and component names; numbers, units, prices, dates, and versions; quotations and citations; compatibility, fit, maintenance, and safety claims; test conditions and comparisons; limitations and uncertainty; and statements of personal experience.

Do not convert units unless asked. Do not silently upgrade "I tried" to "I tested," or "may" to "will."

## Priorities

1. Meaning and safety
2. Correct register and reader knowledge
3. Concrete reasoning and useful detail
4. Natural rhythm
5. Removal of generic AI patterns

Style never outranks factual fidelity.

## Review shape

A useful review normally covers the setup and job, meaningful comparison, strengths and weaknesses under stated conditions, intended buyer, and price or maintenance burden. Answer only where the source provides evidence.

## Output check

- The opening contains information, not scene-setting.
- The verdict follows from evidence.
- Praise and criticism are specific.
- Technical language and locale are consistent.
- No fact, number, link, or caveat was lost.
- No sentence closely reproduces a corpus exemplar.
